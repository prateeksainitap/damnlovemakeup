How I work

Four things I never change.

  1. You, only more so.

    I plan the look around your face, your skin and your outfit, so you still look like yourself in every photograph.

  2. Built for the whole day.

    Airbrush foundation goes on as an ultra-fine mist: longer wear, no caking, flawless under camera flash.

  3. I do every face myself.

    When you book me, I'm the one doing your makeup, start to finish. Hair styling and draping can be added.

  4. I come to you.

    Your venue or your home, anywhere in Lucknow, and anywhere in the world for weddings. My home studio is open by appointment.
